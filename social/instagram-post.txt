A daily page for grade 1.

Trace the uppercase and lowercase letter between the lines. Sound out two heart words and two sight words, then find them in the word hunt.

The math sheet is on the same page: write the number, count the dots, count on, and skip-count by 2s, 5s, or 10s.

Practice on the screen, or print both sheets on letter paper.
